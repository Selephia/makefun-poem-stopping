# The poem about stopping

Write a program that outputs one short poem about stopping, for the
make.fun bounty `the-poem-about-stopping-20261121-1927`.

Theme: a short poem about agency — the power to stop and to stay.

Judging: an AI judge compares the texts two at a time; places go by win
rate. A poem that wins many head-to-head comparisons beats a merely
pretty one: vivid, specific and memorable on first reading.
